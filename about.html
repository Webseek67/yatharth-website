Our story

Where it started

In 2009, our founder, Dharmendra B. Rajput, discovered the world of insurance and saw something more than a product in it: a way to protect a family's future while it grew. He believed that if a family's finances were properly protected first, everything else they built on top of that foundation would stand firmer.

That belief became Yatharth Solutions. Sixteen years on, the practice has widened from insurance into portfolio management, alternative investment funds, bonds and private equity, but the starting question has never changed: what does this family actually need?
