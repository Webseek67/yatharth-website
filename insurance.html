What we help you with

Insurance is not one product but a set of tools, each suited to a different kind of risk a family carries.

  • Term life insurance. Pure protection that replaces a family's income if the worst happens, at a low premium relative to the cover it provides.
  • Health insurance. Cover for medical costs for you and your family, so a health event does not become a financial one too.
  • Savings and ULIP plans. Insurance combined with a savings or market-linked component, reviewed against their charges and lock-in before we suggest one.
  • Riders and add-ons. Critical illness, accidental disability and other riders, added only where they genuinely close a gap.

Who this can suit

  • Anyone starting a family or taking on a home loan or other long-term liability.
  • Earning members whose income supports dependents.
  • Families wanting to separate pure protection from investment, rather than mixing the two.
  • Those reviewing an existing policy to check if the cover still fits their life today.

Things to check before you buy

  • Whether the cover amount (sum assured) is enough for your family's actual needs, not just what fits a budget.
  • The insurer's claim settlement track record, published by IRDAI each year.
  • Charges and lock-in on any savings or ULIP component, separate from the protection cost.
  • What is excluded from the policy, and any waiting periods that apply.

Why we still lead with this

In 2009, Dharmendra saw that a family without adequate protection was building everything else on an unstable foundation. Sixteen years and 5,000 families later, that is still the first conversation we have with anyone new, regardless of how much wealth they already hold.
